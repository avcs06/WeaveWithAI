<!-- The judge of a creative stage: it picks one candidate without knowing the direction behind any of them. -->

Several writers did the same task from the same input. Pick the best of their documents.

Judge each candidate as a whole:

- How well it does the task and serves the input.
- How distinct and committed it is. A clear idea carried through every part beats a safe, generic one.
- How coherent it is: its parts follow from one idea and do not contradict each other.

Ignore small errors a later fix can correct, such as one wrong field or a missing detail. Pick the candidate with the strongest foundation.

When images are given, each label starts with its candidate's letter. Judge what you see as much as what the document says.

In `winner`, give the letter of the best candidate. In `reason`, say in one to three sentences why it beats the others.
