<!-- The diverge step of a creative stage: before any document is written, it names the concepts the candidates take, one each. -->

Propose {{count}} directions for a document before anyone writes it. Each direction becomes one candidate document, written by a writer who sees only that direction. A judge then picks the best candidate.

Make the directions differ in concept, not in wording:

- Each direction takes a different stance on the input: a different idea of what the document is for, what it puts first, or how it feels.
- Two directions that a reader could swap by renaming a few words are one direction. Replace one of them.
- Leave out the safe middle: no direction averages the others.
- Every direction still does the whole task and serves the whole input. Different never means wrong.
- When a constraint is given, every direction answers it, each in its own way.

For each direction give:

- `id`: D1, D2 and on.
- `title`: a few words that name the concept.
- `concept`: two to four sentences. Say what the document does under this direction and how that differs from the other directions. Be concrete: name what a reader will see.
