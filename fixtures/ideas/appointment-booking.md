A booking page for a solo yoga teacher.

The teacher signs in, sets weekly availability (which days, from what time to what
time), defines class types (name, duration, a price shown for information only,
and the maximum number of participants) and sees upcoming bookings.

Students don't have accounts. They open the teacher's public page, pick a class
type, see open slots for the next two weeks, and book with their name and email.
A slot is full when it reaches the class type's maximum. After booking, the student
sees a cancellation link on screen (no emails are sent) and can use it to cancel.

The teacher can cancel a slot, which cancels all its bookings.
